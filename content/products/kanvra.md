problem: Work and routines usually live in separate apps. Tasks scatter across notes, reminders, and chat. Habits sit somewhere else. Opening three tools to plan one day is friction you feel every morning.
solution: Kanvra keeps both in one place. A Kanban board for work. A tracker for routines. Spaces to separate contexts. Everything stays on the iPhone — no account, no ads, no tracking.
sections:
  - title: What it does
    body: Kanvra is built for personal work, not team project management. You run a board with columns like To Do, Doing, and Done. You track the habits you want to keep. Spaces let you keep General, Work, and side projects apart without mixing lists. The app is meant to stay quiet and useful — open it, move a card, check a routine, close it.
  - title: How to use it
    body: Create a space for a context you care about. Add tasks to the board and drag them as work moves. Use the habit tracker for the daily or weekly routines you want to hold. Switch spaces when you change gears. There is nothing to sign into. Reinstalling the app clears local data, so treat the board as something that lives with the device.
  - title: How it was built
    body: Kanvra is a Flutter app for iPhone. The UI and local storage run on device — there is no backend that holds your lists. That choice keeps privacy simple and the product focused. Shipping meant finishing the board, the habit flow, spaces, and the App Store listing with honest support and privacy pages.
