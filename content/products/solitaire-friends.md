sections:
  - title: What it does
    body: Solitaire Friends is classic solitaire for mobile. You resume a game or start a classic deal, move cards with touch, and aim to clear the board. When a hand is finished you see the outcome — time, moves, and score — then you can deal again. The focus is the table, not a social feed or a long setup.
  - title: How to use it
    body: Open the app, choose Resume or Classic Game from the title screen, and play the board. Drag or tap according to the rules of the deal. When you solve a layout, review the result and start another. Game progress stays on your device. If you reinstall, local progress is cleared. On iOS, Apple’s Allow Tracking prompt may appear before ads start — you can allow or ask not to track.
  - title: How it was built
    body: The game is built in Unity with C# for mobile. Version 2.1 wires advertising through Unity LevelPlay so Unity Ads and ironSource Ads can serve and measure ads. Authorized sellers are listed in app-ads.txt on this site. The product side includes the play loop, title and solved screens, on-device progress, and the support and privacy pages required for the Store.
